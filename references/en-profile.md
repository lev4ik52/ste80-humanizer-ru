# English and mixed-language profile

This is a secondary plain-language profile, not an ASD-STE100 implementation.
Do not translate a Russian or mixed-language document unless asked.

Keep claims, numbers, names, qualifications, quotations, commands and URLs.
Remove empty framing and inflated wording only when it carries no information.
Prefer direct verbs and known actors; do not invent an actor to eliminate a passive.
Keep meaningful uncertainty, contractions and the writer's register.

Use sentence length as a review hint, not a hard limit. Keep logical connections.
Preserve intentional rhetorical contrast, real three-part lists, punctuation,
greetings, citations and disclosures. Never add errors to imitate a human.

In mixed RU/EN documents, apply Russian editing to Russian prose and English
editing to English prose. Leave code, product names and technical identifiers alone.
Do not promise approved-dictionary coverage or STE compliance. `strict` means
clear procedural prose in this project, not a validated controlled-language result.

The bundled linter targets Russian. Its few English patterns are convenience
checks, not equivalent English coverage. The editor still performs the full
claim-by-claim review described in the fidelity reference.
